# 🧣 Scout Necker Archive

**Preserving Scout neckerchiefs from around the world.**

The **Scout Necker Archive** is a community-driven digital archive dedicated to documenting, preserving, and identifying Scout neckerchiefs from every country and Scout organization.

Our goal is to create the most comprehensive and accessible collection of Scout neckerchiefs while preserving an important part of Scouting history for future generations.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 Worldwide Scout neckerchief collection
- 🔎 Advanced search and filtering
- 📖 Detailed neckerchief information
- 📸 High-quality photographs
- 🤝 Community submissions
- 🏛️ Carefully reviewed archive entries
- 📱 Responsive design
